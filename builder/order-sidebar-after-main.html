Saving tomato seed in October

The last trusses ripened late this year, which turned out to be a good thing: the fruit we kept for seed had a full month on the vine.

Scoop the seeds with their jelly into a jar, add a splash of water and leave it on a warm shelf for three days. The mould that forms on top breaks down the coating that stops seeds sprouting inside the fruit.

Rinse, spread on a plate, and let them dry for a week before you label the envelope.
